K2  threshold-crossing rate  E[1{flip}] for noise sd = sqrt(eps)
    P(flip | distance d to threshold) = Phi(-|d|/sqrt(eps))
    E_d[.] with density p near 0  =  sqrt(eps) * 2 * E[Z_+] * p(0) + O(eps)
    2 E[Z_+] = 2/sqrt(2 pi) = 0.797885
    => the leading eps-response is O(sqrt(eps)) = O(noise SD), NOT O(eps).
